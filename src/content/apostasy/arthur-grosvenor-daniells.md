### The 1904 Crime Against the Pioneer Faith

On **April 15, 1904**, Daniells signed the Articles of Incorporation forming the **General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists** in the District of Columbia — the "new organization" Ellen White had specifically warned against in **Selected Messages, Book 1, p. 204**:

> *"A new organization would be established. Books of a new order would be written. A system of intellectual philosophy would be introduced… Our religion would be changed."*

EGW had also written in **Selected Messages, Book 2, p. 390**:

> *"We cannot now enter into any new organization; for this would mean apostasy from the truth."*

Daniells ignored both warnings and proceeded to incorporate the Adventist movement as a **501(c)(3)-eligible federal corporation**, permanently marrying the church to the state.

### Catholic School Accreditation

One of Daniells' most consequential — and least discussed — actions was his push to join government-regulated accreditation bodies for SDA schools. Under his administration:

- SDA academies and colleges were brought under **regional accreditation frameworks** dominated by Catholic-influenced educational standards
- Schools were required to adopt **curriculum compatible with state and Roman educational systems**, gradually replacing the **true Protestant and health-reform education principles of the pioneers**
- **Donors were told schools were Protestant** — this was the beginning of what 144000.com calls **"religious donation fraud"**
- Over 7,500 SDA schools worldwide now operate under this accreditation system, teaching **the Trinity (Catholic Catechism #234)**, union of church and state doctrine, and ecumenical partnership theology — while fundraising as *Protestant* institutions

> *"The time is coming when in their fraud and insolence men will reach a point that the Lord will not permit them to pass, and they will learn that there is a limit to the forbearance of Jehovah."* — Testimonies for the Church, Vol. 9, p. 13 (1909)

### IRS Filing vs. Catholic Confession

The corporation Daniells created in 1904 was eventually recognized by the IRS in 1950 as a **501(c)(3) Protestant organization** (X21 religious classification). Yet under his institutional successors, GC President **Neal C. Wilson** would later confess in federal court (1974) and in print (1981):

> *"There is another universal and truly Catholic organization, the Seventh-day Adventist Church."* — Adventist Review, March 5, 1981

Daniells laid the structural groundwork for this identity fraud. He built the house; others confirmed what it truly was.
