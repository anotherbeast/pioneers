## Resume of Apostasy

**Erton Carlos Köhler** became the **21st President of the General Conference of Seventh-day Adventists** on July 4, 2025, when global delegates voted 1,721 to 188 (90.15%) at the **62nd GC Session in St. Louis, Missouri**. He succeeded Ted N.C. Wilson after three consecutive Wilson terms.

Köhler does not inherit a pioneer church. He inherits the **General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists** — the federal corporate entity incorporated in Washington, D.C., on **April 15, 1904** — the "new organization" Ellen White identified as apostasy from the truth.

### What He Inherited

On July 4, 2025, Köhler stepped into the presidency of an organization with the following legal and institutional identity:

- **Federal corporation** incorporated April 15, 1904, Washington D.C.
- **IRS 501(c)(3) status** (EIN on record), classified as Protestant (X21 religious classification)
- **Institutional founder**: A.G. Daniells — whose administration EGW condemned at the 1901 Battle Creek GC Session
- **Documented Catholic confession**: Neal C. Wilson (GC President 1979–1990) declared in the *Adventist Review* (March 5, 1981): *"There is another universal and truly Catholic organization, the Seventh-day Adventist Church."*
- **7,500+ accredited schools** brought under government-regulated educational frameworks initiated under Daniells and Prescott

The organization Köhler heads remains, under law, the direct successor to the April 15, 1904 incorporation.

### The 2025 St. Louis Election

Köhler was elected with an overwhelming 90.15% approval by global delegates — a margin that reflects not doctrinal unanimity but **institutional momentum**. He is the first South American to lead the global church, which carries significant symbolic weight in a denomination now majority non-Western in membership.

His stated priorities include mission, unity, and faithfulness — the same priorities stated by every GC president since 1904. The question is not personal sincerity. The question is whether the **legal and institutional structure** he now heads can produce genuine reform, or whether the 1904 framework will continue unchanged under Brazilian leadership.

### The EGW Test

Ellen White's warning applies with equal force to 2025 as to 1904:

> *"We cannot now enter into any new organization; for this would mean apostasy from the truth."*
> — 2 Selected Messages, p. 390

The corporation was entered into in 1904. Every GC president since Daniells has continued it. Köhler has inherited it.

> *"A new organization would be established. Books of a new order would be written. A system of intellectual philosophy would be introduced… Our religion would be changed."*
> — 1 Selected Messages, p. 204

The question for the Köhler era is whether this trajectory will continue or be publicly acknowledged and reversed.

### Verdict

Erton Köhler is not personally implicated in the 1904 crime — he was born 65 years after the signing. But he is the **current legal president** of the Apr. 15, 1904 corporate entity. His administration either continues the apostasy structure or dismantles it.

**History will record which one he chose.**
