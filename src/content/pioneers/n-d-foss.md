## N.D. Foss (1835–1908)

N.D. Foss was a dedicated evangelist and missionary whose evangelistic campaigns and strategic mission work expanded Seventh-day Adventism into new regions. His combination of revivalist preaching and missionary vision proved effective in movement expansion.

## Early Evangelistic Career

- **Born**: New York, 1835
- **Call**: Felt strong evangelistic calling
- **Training**: Prepared for evangelistic ministry
- **Early Campaigns**: Began tent meetings and revivals

- Revival results
