## E.P. Wiles (1835–1910)

E.P. Wiles was an Adventist evangelist and colporteur whose pioneering literature-based evangelism established colporteur ministry as distinct missionary approach. His commitment to distributing Adventist literature combined with personal evangelism created effective model for literature-based outreach.
