## Stephen Pierce (1806–1880)

Stephen Pierce was an early Seventh-day pastoral leader whose ministry helped establish the Sabbath truth within the emerging Adventist movement. His long pastoral career spanned from the Millerite era through Seventh-day Adventism's organizational formation.

## Early Life & Call

- **Born**: New York, 1806
- **Religious Heritage**: Christian background
- **Pastoral Call**: Felt called to pastoral ministry
- **Faith Journey**: Religious leader seeking deeper truth

## Millerite Involvement

**1844 Movement**:
- Embraced William Miller's advent expectation
- Participated in Millerite gatherings
- Preached about prophetic expectation
- Experienced Great Disappointment

**Faith Perseverance**:
- Maintained faith after 1844
- Continued pastoral ministry
- Sought deeper biblical understanding
- Open to new revelations

## Seventh-day Discovery

**Sabbath Truth**:

**Pastoral Ministry**:

## Legacy

Stephen Pierce exemplifies the pastoral leaders who quietly sustained faith during transitions. While perhaps less prominent than visionary figures, such pastors provided essential stability and spiritual guidance. His consistent pastoral care enabled spiritual community to survive 1844 disappointment and embrace new truth.
