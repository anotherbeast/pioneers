# The 1850 Prophecy Chart

The 1850 Prophecy Chart is a foundational Adventist document, created with the direct involvement of Otis Nichols and Mary Bird Nichols, and endorsed by Ellen White as being "directed by the hand of the Lord."

## Historical Context

- **Produced:** Spring 1850, Boston, Massachusetts
- **Key Contributors:** Otis Nichols, Mary Bird Nichols, James White, Ellen White
- **Purpose:** To visually present the Three Angels’ Messages (Revelation 14), the 2300-day prophecy, and the call to come out of Babylon

## Ellen White’s Endorsement

> "I saw that the truth should be made plain upon tables, that the earth and the fullness thereof is the Lord’s, and that necessary means should not be spared to make it plain. I saw that the charts ordered by God should be used..."  
> — Ellen White, Early Writings, p. 74

Ellen White never gave such an endorsement to any later chart. The 1850 chart stands alone as the visual summary of the Advent movement’s prophetic understanding.

## Chart Features

- The 2300-day prophecy (Daniel 8:14)
- The Three Angels’ Messages (Revelation 14)
- The Second Angel’s Message: "Babylon is fallen"
- The Midnight Cry
- Key Millerite and Adventist prophetic symbols
