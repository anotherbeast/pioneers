# Blasphemy Against the Holy Spirit: The Ultimate Warning

## What Is the Blasphemy of the Holy Spirit?

> "The sin against the Holy Spirit is the persistent rejection of light and truth. It is not a single act, but a firm, determined resistance of truth and evidence, a persistent refusal to respond to the invitation to repent."  
> — Ellen G. White, *The Desire of Ages*, p. 322 (1898)

> "It is not God that hardens the heart of men, but the resistance of His grace, the rejection of the warning of mercy, is the cause of the fatal hardness."  
> — Ellen G. White, *Steps to Christ*, p. 33 (1892)

> "The blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is the willful, determined resistance of truth and evidence, a persistent refusal to respond to the invitation to repent."  
> — Ellen G. White, *Testimonies for the Church*, vol. 5, p. 634 (1889)

> "The sin of blasphemy against the Holy Spirit does not lie in any sudden word or deed. It is the firm, determined resistance of truth and evidence."  
> — Ellen G. White, *The Desire of Ages*, p. 322 (1898)

## The Bible on the Unpardonable Sin

> "Wherefore I say unto you, All manner of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men: but the blasphemy against the Holy Ghost shall not be forgiven unto men. And whosoever speaketh a word against the Son of man, it shall be forgiven him: but whosoever speaketh against the Holy Ghost, it shall not be forgiven him, neither in this world, neither in the world to come."  
> — Matthew 12:31-32, KJV

> "Quench not the Spirit."  
> — 1 Thessalonians 5:19, KJV

> "He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy."  
> — Proverbs 29:1, KJV

## The 1904 SDA New Organization: A Case Study in Rejection

> "The result of the formation of this new organization would be apostasy from the truth. Warnings have been given, but the warnings have been disregarded. The result is seen in the present condition of the church."  
> — Ellen G. White, *Selected Messages*, vol. 1, p. 204 (1904)

> "The new organization will wax stronger and stronger in apostasy until the Lord shall descend from heaven with a shout."  
> — Ellen G. White, Letter 242, April 15, 1904

## The Danger of Persistent Rejection

> "It is not that God sends out a decree that man shall not be saved, but man closes his heart against the Holy Spirit, and the persistent rejection of the Spirit's pleading leads to the loss of the power to repent and be saved."  
> — Ellen G. White, *The Desire of Ages*, p. 324 (1898)

> "The sin against the Holy Spirit is the sin of persistent refusal to respond to the invitation to repent."  
> — Ellen G. White, *The Desire of Ages*, p. 322 (1898)

## A Call to Respond

The blasphemy against the Holy Spirit is not a single act, but a process—a repeated, willful rejection of Christ’s Spirit, His warnings, and His truth. The 1904 new organization and its continued apostasy are a living warning. Today, the invitation is still open: "Today if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts" (Hebrews 3:15, KJV).

**Will you respond to the Spirit’s call, or will you join the ranks of those who persistently resist until the voice of God is no longer heard?**
